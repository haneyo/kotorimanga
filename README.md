# Kotori Manga

A clean, modern manga reader for iOS. Browse your local library, connect to network storage, and read with a streamlined interface built for manga.

## Features

### Bookshelf
A gorgeous grid-based bookshelf with flexible layouts. Switch between 2x2, 3x3, list view, and more. Smart sorting, folder organization, search, multi-select, and recent reading history.

### Flexible Reading Modes
Read manga your way with single page, double page, and strip (webtoon) modes. Tap or swipe page turning with LTR/RTL support, volume button page turn, and auto bookmarks.

### Import from Anywhere
Import from local files, Wi-Fi drag & drop transfer, or connect to cloud services and network servers — SMB, WebDAV, FTP, OPDS, Google Drive, Dropbox, Mega, iCloud, Box — all supported natively. Supports ZIP, CBZ, EPUB, PDF, RAR, CBR, and more.

### Built-in Manga Browser
Browse manga websites directly within the app. The intelligent detector automatically finds manga images on any page, letting you download entire chapters to your shelf with a single tap.

### Wi-Fi Transfer
Transfer manga between your computer and device over Wi-Fi. Open a browser on any device on the same network to upload manga files, or export manga from your shelf. No cables, accounts, or extra software needed.

### Privacy First
All processing happens on-device. No account required, no tracking, no data collection. Your manga, your privacy.
